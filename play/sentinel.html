Tap one of your pieces to select it, then tap a highlighted square. A single stone shows its forward steps (dashed circles outside the board are moves off the board). A stack shows the squares it would sow along each direction; tap any of them to sow that way, the larger dot marks where the last stone lands. Tap the selected piece again to deselect it (Esc also clears). Numbers on stones are stack heights. With "Show lines of sight" on, lines join the center to every piece that sees it. Red dots mark the last move.
