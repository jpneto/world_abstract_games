HexSprawl

Click an empty hex to place a stack, or one of your stacks to add a piece; then click the landing hexes for its sprawl. Dashed stacks are unsupported and would be removed on their owner’s turn. Green dots: the opponent cannot capture the stack there next turn; red dots: he can. A gold ring and light-yellow arrows mark the last move, by either player (arrows run from the sprawling stack’s old hex to each landing hex).
