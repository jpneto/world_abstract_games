Each turn has two parts. First drop: tap an intersection (empty, or one of your stacks) to drop a piece from your reserve, and tap it again for each extra piece, up to four in a stack. The drop is played as soon as you do anything else. Then, optionally, sow a different stack: tap it to pick up all its pieces (tap it again before laying to pick up one fewer), then tap the highlighted intersections one by one to lay one piece on each; the sowing is played when every piece in hand is laid (a stack of four may come back round onto its own point). Press "Skip sowing" to end the turn without sowing; "Cancel" (the Undo button) or Esc cancels a pending drop or sowing, and Undo takes back moves. Numbers on stones are stack sizes (a plain stone is a single piece). Light grey rings mark the stones played in the last turn (the drop and every point of the sowing; a dashed ring is where the sowing started), and red crosses mark where pieces were captured.
