Click an empty hex of your base edge to place a piece, or click one of your supported pieces to see where it can go: a filled dot means the piece is still supported there and may keep moving, a red ring means it stops there unsupported and will be lost. “Remove 2 + 1” sacrifices two of your supported pieces to take one enemy piece. Dashed pieces are unsupported and are lost if still unsupported at the end of their owner’s turn. Tinted edges: solid tint = base, dashed outline = terminal.
