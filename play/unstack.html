Click one of your stacks, then click squares one at a time to lay out the sowing path: each click on a new adjacent square drops one piece there, and clicking the path's last square again drops one more. Click an earlier path square to cut the path back to it, or the stack itself to cancel. Press Sow to play. Dots mark squares the path can extend to, rings mark captures, and dashed rings mark stacks that can capture (captures are mandatory). Tinted squares show the last move.
