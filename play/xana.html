Grey dots mark the cells accessible to the player to move. Set how many stones to drop, then click a dotted cell to drop them. Or click one of your stacks: purple dots then show its circle; click one to move the chosen number of stones there, or use Add on top to drop stones from your reserve onto it. After a drop or move, red dots mark where walls can go: click up to two, or press End turn. Tinted cells show the last turn. A small Gold or Blue square marks an empty cell only that player can access; cells neither player can access are filled black.
