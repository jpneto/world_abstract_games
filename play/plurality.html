Tap empty intersections to pick your tromino. Your first taps are your own stones; tap a picked intersection again to make it the opponent's stone, and once more to remove it. Once two of yours are picked, the next tap becomes the opponent's stone. The move is played as soon as the three picked intersections form a legal tromino with exactly one opponent stone (Esc clears the pick). Orange dots show where the pick can be completed. With "Show territories" on, small squares mark the empty intersections of each player's scoring territories (during play, only areas where no legal tromino fits any more; at the end, all owned areas). Red dots mark the last move.
