Click a pile you control (your colour on top), then click where it goes: a pile moves as many squares as the pieces you lift, so the distance picks how many come off the top. Green dots mark the squares you can reach. To enter a piece from your reserve, click your reserve tray in the cut-off corner, then any square. Dashed rings mark what you can move. Piles are drawn from the side, bottom piece lowest, with their height. The gold outline marks the last move. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces (moves are entered the same way).
