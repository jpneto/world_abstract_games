Click an empty cell to grow a sprout next to one of your plants or parasites; switch to “Place: seed” to grow or sow a seed. Small dots show where the current kind of piece may go. Click one of your pieces to select it: orange dots are the cells a sprout can move to, purple rings the landing cells of a parasite (only for the centre of a button), and “Prune / harvest” removes the selected sprout or seed. Seeds have a white ring, parasites a dashed outline. Blue edges wrap round (top touches bottom); red edges are walls. Gold rings mark every piece placed or moved during the turn being played (before its first action, during the previous turn), and small gold dots the cells they came from or the pieces removed.
