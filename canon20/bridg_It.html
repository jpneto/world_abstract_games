Click a crossing point between two of your posts to build a bridge there; hovering shows where it would go. Blue’s bridges and Red’s bridges share the same centre points, so a taken point blocks the other player. In the two-bridge variant, green dots mark the legal crossing points; points that would join your first bridge’s group get no dot. The gold outline marks the bridge(s) placed in the last turn. Blue bars mark West and East, red bars North and South.
