Click one of your stacks of two or more stones, then click the points it sows into, one step at a time: each click drops one stone on a diagonally adjacent point along your own lines, and the move ends when the last stone is dropped. A step may not go straight back the way the last step came. Green dots mark the steps that can still finish the move; click the last point you dropped on to take that step back, or the starting stack (before the first step) to cancel. In the part-stack variant, clicking the starting stack before the first step lifts one stone fewer each time. Dashed rings mark the stacks you can move. The gold line and rings mark the last move and the stacks it captured. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces (moves are entered the same way).
