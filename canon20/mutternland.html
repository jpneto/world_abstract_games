The small number on each stack is its height; a tower (four nuts) has a dark rim, and dashed outlines are positions with no nut (never laid, or a stack that disappeared). During setup, click an empty nut on the edge of the surface (orange dots). On your turn, click one of your marbles: orange dots are the nuts it can move or jump to, purple rings the enemy marbles it can push (alone or as the rear of a line). Then click a free stack next to one of your marbles (hollow dots) and an orange dot to move one nut there. Clicking a nut before moving a marble skips the marble step. “Place returned marble” puts back a marble that was pushed off. Gold rings mark every marble placed or moved and every stack that received a nut during the turn being played (before its first action, during the previous turn); small gold dots mark the cells they came from.
