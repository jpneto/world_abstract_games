Click one of your pieces to select it; dots show where it can go. A hollow ring on a dot means the piece can keep jumping from there: click the next square to continue, or click the piece (or “Stop here”) to end the move. A purple diamond is an exchange square for a double tower on the opponent’s back row (once per game). Every piece wears a grey metal ring; double towers also wear a red ring. The number on a piece is how many rings it carries (shown from two up). Gold marks the last move (its start and end squares); red crosses mark the pieces it captured.
