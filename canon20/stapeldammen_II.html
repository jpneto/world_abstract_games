Click a piece you control (your man on top), then click the square to move to; green dots mark the legal squares. For a multiple capture, click each landing square in turn (the rest is filled in once only one route is left). When a capture is available, only the stacks that can capture are ringed in green. Stacks are drawn from the side, bottom man lowest, and the number shows the stack height (it stays visible with highlights off). The big number next to each player is their current score: the men in the stacks they control on the far row. The gold outline marks the last move’s route. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces (moves are entered the same way).
