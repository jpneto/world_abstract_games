Click an empty cell to place a stone; hovering shows where it would go. Red links the top and bottom edges, Blue the left and right edges (the coloured borders). The gold ring marks the stone(s) placed in the last turn. In Chameleon, choose the colour with the stone buttons; right-clicking a cell places the other colour. In 12* Hex, while you place your second stone, shaded cells would join the first stone’s group and are not allowed.
