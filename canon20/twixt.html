Click an empty hole to place your peg; hovering shows where it would go. With “Auto-link and end turn” ticked, every legal link from the new peg is added and the turn ends at once. Untick it to choose links yourself: click one of your pegs, then one of the dashed-ringed pegs to link them, and press End turn. Click one of your own links to remove it (allowed any time during your turn). Red’s border rows are the top and bottom rows (red lines), Black’s are the left and right columns (black lines); you may not place a peg in the opponent’s border rows. The gold rings and outlines mark the peg and links of the last turn. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces.
