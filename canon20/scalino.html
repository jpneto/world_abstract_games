Pick a block from your tray (colour and length), then click the space where one end of it goes: green dots mark the spaces where an end of that block can go. For a two- or three-space block the possible positions then appear as shaded outlines with a purple ring on the other end; click the ring (or the outline) to place it. Click the selected end again to cancel; clicking a space without a dot explains why the block cannot go there. Blocks of the same colour may touch at a corner but not along a side. Blocks of higher levels are drawn smaller, inside the ones below (or raised, in the 3D view); the numbers give the level of the top block on each space (blocks without a number are on the first level). The gold outline marks the block or blocks placed in the previous turn.
