Click one of your pieces, then a green dot. A piece moves exactly as many squares as there are pieces (of both colours, itself included) on the line it moves along; it may pass over its own pieces but not over enemy pieces, and it captures an enemy piece by landing on it (a dot with a ring). Your pieces that can move get a thin dashed outline. The big number next to each player is their pieces left; the small text counts how many separate groups they have. The gold outlines and line mark the last move. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces (moves are entered the same way).
