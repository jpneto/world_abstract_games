A move takes three clicks: the far end of a line of your pieces, the tower square at its other end, then the last square of the line where the tower is laid out (the tower square keeps one piece). Dashed rings mark where a line can start, green dots the next squares you can pick; click a selected square again to step back. Only the top piece of a stack counts and moves. Stacks are drawn from the side, bottom piece lowest, with their height. The gold outline marks the last move. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces (moves are entered the same way).
