Sticks lie between grid points. Click an empty slot marked with a dot to place a stick there, or click one of your sticks to reposition it (allowed once your reserve is empty, or for sticks inside one of your strongholds). Dots: green = legal, red ring = the stick touches an enemy graph and captures it (and is itself lost), gold = wins. Sticks in the darker red or the gold tone form strongholds and cannot be captured. The red and white bars mark the two defense lines. The gold outline marks the stick placed last and the small gold dot where it came from; a gold dashed slot marks a capturing stick that left the board, and faded dashed sticks are the ones it captured.
