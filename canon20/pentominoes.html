Pick a piece from the reserve, then point at the board: the shaded outline shows where it would go, and a click drops it. Rotate with R, the right mouse button or the “Rotate” button, mirror with F or “Mirror”; clicking the selected piece in the reserve also rotates it. On a touch screen, tap a square to show the piece there and tap the outline again to drop it. Grey dots mark the squares where the selected piece, as it is turned now, can be dropped (the dot is the square under the pointer). A gold outline marks the piece placed last; the small white or dark disc on each piece shows who placed it.
