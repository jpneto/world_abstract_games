Click your manipulator (the tall piece) to see where it can go: purple dots mark its destinations. When two routes lead to the same point, orange rings mark the two corners and you click the one where your pion is placed. Orange rings on your pions show which of them can capture, and red dashed rings the enemy pions they can take; click such a pion, then a purple ring to jump. During a chain of captures the pions already taken are shown faded, and you keep jumping until the chain is complete. Gold rings mark the pieces placed or moved by the last turn, small gold dots the points they left and the pions captured. The coloured bands show which edges each player must join.
