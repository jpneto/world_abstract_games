Click one of your pieces to select it: orange dots are the squares it can step to, purple rings the squares it can hop to (filled when the hop makes a capture). After a hop the same piece may hop again: click another purple ring, or click the piece or “End hop” to stop. Leaders and Inspectors are the large pieces with a white ring. Gold rings mark the piece moved during the turn being played (before its first action, during the previous turn), and small gold dots the squares it left and the pieces it captured. Captured pieces are shown in the Prison and the Hospital.
