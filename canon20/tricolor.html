Stacks are drawn from the side, one disc per piece from bottom to top, so very tall stacks rise into the hexes behind them. The number on the top disc is the stack’s power and the badge gives its height (hover over a stack for its composition). Yellow hexes multiply power by 1, green by 2, red by 3. Click one of your stacks to select it, choose with “Lift” how many pieces to take from the top, then click a destination: orange dots are simple moves, orange rings stacking on your own stack, purple rings captures and red rings kills. When an attack is compulsory, only the stacks able to attack get a dashed ring. Gold marks the previous turn: the stack that moved (outlined top and ring at its foot), a line along its path and a dot where it came from.
