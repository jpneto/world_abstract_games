Click a point holding one of your pieces, then click the point to move to; green dots mark the legal points, and light dashed rings mark the points with a piece that can move. If several of your pieces share the point, click it again to switch between them (the selected piece is outlined in orange). For a capture, click each point of the route in turn (the rest is filled in once only one route is left); when a capture reaches your Goal Line early, click that point again to stop there. Pieces are drawn from the side: the Tower is tall and narrow, the Wall medium, the Moat low and wide. The gold outline marks the last move’s route. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces (moves are entered the same way).
