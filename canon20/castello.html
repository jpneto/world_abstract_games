Click one of your pieces, then a green dot. A dot one square away is a step, two squares away in a straight line over an empty square is a first-move double step, and two squares away over a piece is a jump. After a jump the piece waits on its landing square: click another dot to keep jumping, or click the piece again to end the move (clicking its starting square cancels). Jumped enemies are faded until the move ends. Pieces that can capture right now get a thin dashed ring. The big number next to each player is the castles they have captured (4 wins). The gold outline marks the last move’s route, and gold crosses its captures. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and pieces (moves are entered the same way).
