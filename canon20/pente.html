Click an intersection to place a stone; hovering shows where it would go. Bracketing exactly two enemy stones in a line captures them. The gold ring marks the stone(s) placed in the last turn and gold dashed circles mark where stones were just captured; a light dashed line runs through a winning row. In Penju, small red crosses mark the points that are forbidden to Red; in Super Pente, faint lines through your first stone show where the second may not go. With the tournament rule, the shaded square marks the centre area that Red may not use on its second turn. Letters name the columns (a–s), numbers the rows (1 at the bottom). Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and stones.
