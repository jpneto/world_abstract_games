Click any empty point to place a stone. The gold dot marks the last stone played; when a group links all three sides, the winning group is traced in red. Each corner belongs to both sides that meet there. Points are named by row and letter, as in e5; tick “Show coordinates” to see them. Rows are numbered from the bottom edge: a row is the line of points running parallel to the bottom edge, from the left edge round to the right edge, and its number is printed where it meets the edges. The letters on the empty points count along each row from its left end. Hover over a point to see its full name. With the pie rule on, the second player may answer the first stone with “Swap colours” and take it over.
