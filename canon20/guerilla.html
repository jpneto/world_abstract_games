Each turn has three stages: grow, move, kill. While growing, orange dots mark the points where one of your groups may grow and green dots the points where you may start the new group; growth goes on until no placement is left. Then click a piece of a group of two or more to move that group: purple rings are the points a piece can jump to over a neighbour from its own group. Click another group to move it next, and “End turn” when you are done. Red dashed rings mark the enemy pieces your pieces would kill if the turn ended now; when a choice of kills is needed they get solid red rings. The numbers on the pieces are their strengths. Gold rings mark the pieces placed or moved during the turn being played (before its first action, during the previous turn), small gold dots the points they left and the pieces killed. Red discs are massacre tokens.
