The game's name is Mutternland. The rules are described in the pdf.

The nuts are to be placed randomly over the surface board. Assume a maximum surface of a hexhex7, and randomly distribute the nuts inside. However, the nuts should define a single connected shape (so, no two or more disconnect islands of nuts) and all nuts should have at least two adjacent nuts (to prevent very strange shapes).

About the setup: the marbles must be placed on the edges of the random nut surface.
