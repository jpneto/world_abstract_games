The name of the game is Ringkampf.

Ringkampf is a 1928 Checkers-like game published by Otto Maier Verlag. It is played on a 8x8 square board, with 16 pieces per player, which are placed on the first two rows of each player's side. A metal ring is placed on each piece. Movements and jumps are diagonal. Jumps over enemy stones imply capture, and are multiple. Jumps over friendly are also allowed. If a piece reaches its last row, it becomes a double piece and gets a red ring. If a piece captures a double piece, it becomes a double piece itself by taking over the red ring. The double piece can diagonally slide. If it jumps over a friendly piece, it must move to the immediate next square. The goal is capture the enemy rings. The game ends when a player only has one piece left that cannot capture on the next move. The winner is the player with more rings.

Use Black and White pieces, and red rings.
