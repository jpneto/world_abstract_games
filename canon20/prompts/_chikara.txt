The game's name is Chikara.

The game is played on a grid of points of size 10x13. The player's first line is called the defense line. The defense line are across the larger size of the board (so, size 13).

Each player has 26 sticks of one color. The sticks are placed between the grid points.

Definitions 

    * a stronghold is a sequence of sticks that enclose an area of the board. The smallest area is a 1x1 square, requiring four sticks.
    * a graph is a set of sticks of the same color that are connected to each other via the grid, that are not part of a stronghold.

Rules

Initially, each player places all his 10 initial sticks in a single turn on their defensive lines, either horizontally or vertically. The remaining sticks stay in reserve

On his turn, the player picks a stick from the reserve and places it where it must be connected to another friendly stick already on board.
    If all sticks are already on board, then players must reposition one of their sticks, that again must be connected to another friendly stick
    All enemy graphs that touch the current moved stick are captured; in the event of a capture, the moved stick is also permanently removed from play
    
If a player creates a stronghold, every friendly every inside it can also the repositioned in this same turn (or afterwards)
    Relocating sticks from inside a stronghold might create a new stronghold, and the relocation rules apply again
    
The player that places a friendly stick over the adversary's defense line (so, the stick must be placed horizontally) wins the game.
