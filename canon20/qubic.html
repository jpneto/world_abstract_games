The four levels of the cube are drawn one above the other, level 4 at the top and level 1 at the bottom. Click any empty cell on any level to place a marker; hovering shows where it would go. A line can run along one level, straight down through all four levels, or diagonally through them, one marker per level. The gold point marks the last marker placed; when a player wins, the four winning markers get a white point at their centre.
