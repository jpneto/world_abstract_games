To move, click the rear stone of the group you want to move, then the square its front stone should reach. Every stone of yours lined up in front of the clicked stone, in that direction, moves with it; so to split a phalanx, click the stone where the moving part begins. The numbers beside each player are stones left; “far row” counts stones on the enemy’s home row. Thin dashed rings mark the squares the front stone can reach (red rings are captures). The light yellow arrow shows the last move, from the rear stone’s start to the front stone’s landing square; small crosses mark stones it captured. In Force, a gold star marks a Hero. Untick “Show highlights” to show only the board and stones (moves are entered the same way).
